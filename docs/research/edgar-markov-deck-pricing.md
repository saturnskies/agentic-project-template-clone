# Edgar Markov deck: price guide (2026-11-02)

**Source and method.** Prices are TCGplayer market prices (USD) as of 2026-11-02, read through Scryfall's price feed (it mirrors TCGplayer; tcgplayer.com pages need a browser to show prices). Every link goes to the TCGplayer product page. For each card I took the lowest price across all paper printings, including foil. A `†` marks a printing where the foil is the cheapest. Prices move daily; check the linked page before you buy.

**Name corrections.** Four cards in the list didn't match any real card. I priced these instead: Kalastria Highborn, Kirol, Attentive First-Year, Sanguine Syphoner, and Immersturm Predator.

**Caveats.** Emeritus of Woe and Scheming Silvertongue are cheapest as promo versions. Basic land prices are approximate (many printings sell for about a dime).
