# Edgar Markov deck: price guide, Opus re-check (2026-11-02)

**Deck total at cheapest printings: $119.77** (100 cards, TCGplayer market prices, USD).

## How these prices were made

- **Source.** TCGplayer market price for each printing, read through Scryfall's API (`api.scryfall.com`), which mirrors TCGplayer prices daily and gives each printing's TCGplayer product link. Every card link below goes to the TCGplayer product page of the printing priced.
- **Cheapest printing.** For each card I looked up its Oracle ID, pulled every paper printing (`oracleid:… unique:prints game:paper`), and took the lowest of nonfoil, foil and etched prices. Oversized, gold-bordered (not tournament legal) and memorabilia printings were excluded. `†` = the cheapest printing is a foil.
- **Basics.** Cheapest nonfoil printing of each basic.
- **Searching by Oracle ID** avoids the double-faced pollution the brief warned about: `Sign in Blood` is priced as the real Sign in Blood, not the back of Scheming Silvertongue, and Emeritus of Woe is priced as its own card.
- **Legality.** Every deck card and every recommended upgrade is legal in Commander as of today (checked from Scryfall legality data).
- Prices move daily; a market price can exist with no copies currently listed (see spot-checks). Open the link before you buy.

## Differences from the Sonnet guide

Compared with [`edgar-markov-deck-pricing.md`](edgar-markov-deck-pricing.md) (same date, same Scryfall feed):

- **Prices agree** for every card both guides priced the same way. Both used identical printings and prices, including Edgar Markov INR #428 at $29.08.
- **Dracula, Blood Immortal:** Sonnet priced the Dracula-named showcase (VOW #334, $1.96). It is the same card as Falkenrath Forebear, whose cheapest printing is $0.31. That is why this deck total is $1.65 lower than Sonnet's $121.42.
- **Mind's Eye recommended as an upgrade:** Sonnet lists Mind's Eye (MSC #288, $0.32) as a budget upgrade. That printing *is* the deck's Panther Idol (same TCGplayer product, 698221), so the deck already runs it.
- **Banned cards recommended:** Sonnet's premium tier includes **Mana Crypt** and **Jeweled Lotus**, both banned in Commander since September 2024. Left out here.
- **Card names:** Sonnet's tables kept the misspelled names (Calastria, Chiral, Emmerstorm, Sanguine Siphoner) and did not catch Null Priest → Nullpriest, Blood-Thief → Bloodthief, or Moogle's → Moogles'. The prices it gave were for the right cards.
- **Sections:** Sonnet filed Village Rites (a sorcery) under Artifacts, Swiftfoot Boots and Talisman of Hierarchy (artifacts) under Enchantments, and Sorin (a planeswalker) under Creatures, so its section subtotals don't match card types. Its deck total is unaffected apart from the Dracula difference.
- **Listings:** Sonnet does not mention that the cheapest Edgar printing had no copies listed.
- **Upgrade picks:** Sonnet's Legion Loyalist, Aetherflux Reservoir, Cabal Coffers + Urborg (8 Swamps is too few to make Coffers worth it), Mana Confluence and Imperial Seal were dropped. Added instead: Cruel Celebrant, Viscera Seer, Indulgent Aristocrat, Bloodthrone Vampire, Falkenrath Aristocrat, Patron of the Vein, Olivia Voldaren, Vito, Exquisite Blood, Shared Animosity, Vault of the Archangel, Damn, Path of Ancestry, Signets, Cavern of Souls, Necropotence and Ancient Tomb. These are more Vampire and aristocrats pieces.
